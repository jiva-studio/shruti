**Ask Sadhu** is an AI assistant that searches the lectures, audio and books
for you and answers in plain language, with citations back to the original
sources. Ask anything — "what does sadhana mean?", "how to start chanting
japa?", "find me a lecture about humility", "show me Bhagavad-gītā 2.13" —
and the assistant looks through the material and replies with a short answer
plus quotes you can listen to.

## What it does

- **Searches by meaning**, not just by keyword — phrasing your question in
  your own words is fine.
- **Quotes the source.** Each citation is a tap-able link that opens the
  exact moment in the original lecture so you can hear the answer for
  yourself.
- **Finds verses and prepares PDFs** — it can pull up a specific shloka, or
  prepare a lecture's transcript as a document when you ask.
- **Points to where something is** — ask "which canto has the Prahlada
  story" and it answers with the address rather than a retelling.
- **Answers from the lecturers you choose.** Say "answer only from
  Prabhupāda's lectures" and every following answer is built from them
  until you say "use all speakers". Books are never narrowed this way —
  scripture is scripture whoever is speaking. Teachers only your own
  library knows work here too.
- **Knows your listening.** Ask what you played this week, or for a
  recap of the lecture you have open.
- **Suggests what to listen to next**, based on what you have already
  heard.
- **Adds lectures from the web (PRO)** — paste a YouTube link, or ask it
  to look for a teacher's talks online, and it offers them for your
  personal library. Once added, they are searched alongside the catalog
  and only you can see them.
- **Suggests follow-up questions** at the end of each answer to help you
  explore the topic further.
- **Remembers the conversation** within a session, so you can refine your
  question step by step.

## Using chat

- Tap **+** at the top to start a fresh conversation.
- Tap the **history** icon to switch between earlier conversations or search
  them by keyword.
- Long-press a citation to save it as a note or add the full lecture to your
  playlist.

## Limits

The assistant only quotes lectures already in the catalog and may
occasionally rephrase imperfectly — always treat citations as the source of
truth and open the original lecture when something matters. A daily message
quota applies; if you hit it, the input is paused until the quota resets. A
PRO subscription raises the daily limit, and asking the assistant for help
with the app does not count against it.
