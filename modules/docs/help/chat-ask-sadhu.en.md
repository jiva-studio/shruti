**Ask Sadhu** is an AI assistant that searches the corpus of lectures for you
and answers in plain language, with citations back to the original
recordings. Ask anything — "what does sadhana mean?", "how to start chanting
japa?", "find me a lecture about humility" — and the assistant looks through
the transcripts and replies with a short answer plus a list of quotes you
can listen to.

## What it does

- **Searches every lecture by meaning**, not just by keyword — phrasing your
  question in your own words is fine.
- **Quotes the source.** Each citation is a tap-able link that opens the
  exact moment in the original lecture so you can hear the answer for
  yourself.
- **Suggests follow-up questions** at the end of each answer to help you
  explore the topic further.
- **Remembers the conversation** within a session, so you can refine your
  question step by step.

## Using chat

- Tap **+** at the top to start a fresh conversation.
- Tap the **history** icon to switch between earlier conversations or search
  them by keyword.
- Long-press a citation to save it as a note or add the full lecture to your
  playlist.

## Limits

The assistant only quotes lectures already in the catalog and may
occasionally rephrase imperfectly — always treat citations as the source of
truth and open the original lecture when something matters. A daily request
quota applies; if you hit it, the input is paused until the quota resets.
