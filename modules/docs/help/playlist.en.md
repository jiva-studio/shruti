The **Home** tab is your playlist — the lectures you are listening to,
under the **Up Next** heading. It works fully offline.

## Adding lectures

- From **Search**, open a lecture and tap **Add to playlist**.
- From an **Ask Sadhu** answer, long-press a citation and add the full
  lecture.

Whatever you add downloads for offline listening automatically — this is
free and does not need a subscription. When you are signed out the app
starts you with a few featured lectures so the playlist is never empty.

## Playing and progress

- **Tap a track** to play; it resumes from where you left off.
- The **ring** around a track shows your saved position, and fills in real
  time for the track currently playing.
- A **double check** marks a lecture you finished from start to end.
- A collection added as a set shows a single ring for the whole group —
  the average progress across its lectures.

## Managing the playlist

- **Remove** a lecture by swiping its row and tapping delete. It is
  archived, not erased — you can find and re-add it from Search.
- Lectures stay in the **order you added them**; there is no manual
  reordering.

## Continuous playback (PRO)

Turn on **Autoplay** (Settings → Appearance) and the next lecture in the
playlist starts automatically when one ends, even with the screen locked.
This is part of the PRO subscription. Without it, playback stops at the end
of each lecture.
