**Sadhana** is the steady, daily spiritual practice that anchors a devotee's life.
Listening to lectures from a spiritual master is one of the most accessible forms
of sadhana: it does not require special conditions, can fit into a walk or the
morning routine, and gradually reshapes the way we think.

The hard part is not finding a lecture — it is showing up every day.

## How the app supports your sadhana

- **Activity tracker** records every minute you listen and turns it into a
  heatmap on the home screen. You can see at a glance which days you practiced
  and which days you skipped.
- **Day streak** counts how many days in a row you've listened to at least one
  lecture. Even a short session counts — what matters is the rhythm, not the
  duration.
- **Daily reminder** sends one notification at the time you choose, gently
  nudging you back to your practice if the day got busy.
- **Playlist with progress** keeps the lecture you were listening to ready
  exactly where you left off, so resuming takes one tap.

The goal of these tools is not to gamify your spiritual life — it is to remove
small frictions so the practice itself stays in focus.
