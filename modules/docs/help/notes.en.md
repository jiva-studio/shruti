**Notes** let you save passages from lectures — a sentence, a paragraph,
or a longer excerpt — so you can come back to them later, share them, or
listen to just that fragment again.

## Creating a note

- Open the **transcript** of a lecture (tap the player body while it is
  playing).
- **Select a range of text** by long-pressing on a sentence and dragging
  the handles to cover the passage you want.
- Tap **Save as note**. The note remembers both the text and the audio
  range, so you can replay the exact excerpt later.
- You can also save a note from a **chat citation** by long-pressing a
  quote in an Ask Sadhu answer.

## The Notes tab

The Notes tab lists every saved excerpt with its source lecture. From
here you can:

- **Tap a note** to open the inline audio player and listen to just that
  fragment.
- **Search** notes by typing in the box at the top.
- **Share** a note as text or as an audio clip — long-press to open the
  action sheet.
- **Delete** a note when you no longer need it.

With a PRO subscription, **Studio** can also turn a note into a short
video to share — long-press a note and choose **Open in Studio**.

## Privacy

Notes are stored locally on your device and synced as part of your
personal data. They never appear in search results visible to other
users, and they are included in **Export** so you can carry them between
devices.
