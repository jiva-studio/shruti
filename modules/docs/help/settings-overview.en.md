The Settings screen is grouped by purpose. Here is what every group and item
controls.

## Account

- **Sign in** — continue with Google (and Apple on iOS) to keep your
  playlist, notes and listening progress synced across devices. Tap again
  while signed in to sign out.
- **Subscription** — your "Shruti" PRO subscription. Tap to open
  the paywall when you are not subscribed, or to manage it when you are.
- **Preferred server** — which content server the app downloads from. Pick a
  closer region if downloads feel slow.
- **Delete account** — remove your account from our servers; see the
  *Deleting your account* help page.

## Library

- **Lecture languages** — choose which languages of lectures appear across
  search, topics and recommendations. This filters the catalog; it does not
  translate anything.

## Appearance

- **Language** — the language of the app interface. Changing it switches all
  labels but does not affect the language of the lectures themselves.
- **Player progress** — show or hide the progress ring around the play
  button in the floating player.
- **Player on notes page** — show an inline audio player next to each saved
  quote.
- **Highlight sentence** — highlight the current spoken sentence while a
  transcript is open.
- **Automatic scroll** *(PRO)* — keep the current paragraph in view as the
  audio plays.
- **Autoplay** *(PRO)* — when a lecture ends, start the next one in your
  playlist automatically.
- **Open transcript automatically** — open the transcript when a lecture
  starts, if one is available.
- **Track info** *(PRO)* — choose which detail (reference, author, location,
  date) sits on the top line under each lecture title.

## Ask Sadhu

- **Chat language** — the language the assistant answers in.
- **Translate quotes** — translate the cited passages into the chat
  language as well.

## Sadhana

- **Activity tracker** — show the listening heatmap on the home screen.
- **Notifications** — enable the daily reminder.
- **Reminder time** — the time of day the reminder fires (visible only when
  notifications are on).
- **Smart library** *(PRO)* — auto-update your playlist and clean up after
  listening; see the *Smart library* help page.

## Data

- **Export user data** — save a backup file with your playlist, notes,
  downloads and progress.
- **Import user data** — restore from a backup. This **replaces** the data
  currently in the app. See the *Export and import* help page.

## Help and contacts

- **Help** — this help dialog.
- **Privacy Policy** — what we collect and how to delete your account.
- **Contact us** — email support (and our VK / Telegram communities in the
  Russian interface).

## About

At the bottom you can see the app version, build ID, content database
version and the active server (CDN) — handy when reporting a problem.
Tapping this area a few times unlocks a hidden debug section.
