The **Search** tab is where you discover lectures and add them to your
playlist. It is a catalog browser, not a question box — to ask a question in
your own words and get an answer, use **Ask Sadhu** instead.

## Ways to find a lecture

- **Search box.** Type a few words from the title or subject. Results are
  drawn from the catalog and limited to your chosen lecture languages
  (Settings → Library).
- **Recommended for you.** A shelf of lectures picked from what you have
  listened to.
- **Collections.** Curated sets of lectures grouped by theme, series or
  event. Tap a collection to open it; tap **See all** on a group to browse
  every collection in it.
- **Topics.** Subject tiles (shown as `#hashtags`). Tap one to see every
  lecture on that topic. Shelves of your most-listened topics also appear
  further down.
- **All lectures.** The full catalog as a flat list, reachable from the
  **Lectures** section.

## Filters

Open the filter to narrow any list. You can combine:

- **Authors, locations, sources, tags, topics, languages** — pick one or
  more of each.
- **Duration** — up to 15 minutes, 15–30, 30–60, or 60+ minutes.
- **Dates** — a from/to range.
- **Sort** — newest first, oldest first, or by scriptural reference.

The same filter powers the Smart Library buffer (Settings → Sadhana).

## Adding a lecture

Tap a lecture to open its actions, then **Add to playlist**. The lecture
appears on your Home playlist and starts downloading for offline listening
right away — no subscription needed. A track already in your playlist shows
a green check; a finished one shows a double check. See the *Track
indicators* page for what every badge means.
