# Notifications and reminders

The app sends **one** local notification per day — a gentle reminder to keep
up your practice. There are no marketing pushes, no server-side notifications,
and no tracking.

## Turning the reminder on

1. Open **Settings → Sadhana**.
2. Toggle **Notifications** on. The system will ask you for permission the
   first time you enable it.
3. The **Reminder time** row appears. Tap it and pick the time of day that
   suits your routine.

If you deny the system permission, the toggle stays off and no notification
is scheduled.

## How the schedule works

- The reminder fires at the chosen local time, every day.
- Only one notification is ever queued. Changing the time replaces the
  previous schedule — it does not create a second reminder.
- The next occurrence is computed each time you change the setting, so the
  schedule always reflects what you see on screen.
- The app does not need to be running. The OS handles the trigger.

## What the notification says

The notification is simple: a short line inviting you back to a lecture.
Tapping it opens the app.

## If you stop seeing reminders

Most often this is the OS doing power saving. Check:

- The **system Settings → Notifications** screen — Lectorium notifications
  must be allowed.
- Battery optimization for Lectorium on Android — putting the app in the
  "unrestricted" category fixes most cases.
- Do not disturb / focus modes — they suppress everything, including this
  reminder.
