Shruti is a collection of lectures that help you study and understand the Bhagavad-gita, Srimad-Bhagavatam and other Vedic scriptures.

Deep ideas in plain words — the soul, consciousness, karma and the meaning of life. Listen on your commute, on a walk or at home, and come back to what matters most whenever you like.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• A large library of lectures, always at hand
• The lecture text alongside the audio — read and listen at the same time
• Bookmark the thoughts that matter and return to them later
• Search lectures by topic
• Download lectures to listen offline
• Ask Sadhu a question and get a clear answer with links to the lectures

A few minutes a day, and the wisdom of the ancient scriptures becomes part of your life.
