Shruti brings a curated library of recorded spiritual lectures together with the sacred texts they explain — so you can listen, read along, and go deeper, all in one simple app.

Hear experienced teachers unfold the timeless wisdom of the Bhagavad-gita, Srimad-Bhagavatam and other Vedic scriptures, then follow along with the transcript and keep the passages that speak to you.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Listen to a growing library of recorded lectures from sadhus
• Follow along with the transcript as you listen
• Bookmark and save the thoughts that matter most
• Search and discover lectures by topic, teacher, source and more
• Download lectures to listen offline, anywhere
• Read the sacred texts — Bhagavad-gita, Srimad-Bhagavatam and more — with original verses, translations and commentary
• Ask Sadhu: get clear answers grounded in the lectures and scriptures, with references you can open

Whether you have five minutes or an hour, Shruti helps you make spiritual study a part of every day.
