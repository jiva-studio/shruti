# Support

_Shruti_

Need help with the app, found a bug, or have a feature request? We're happy to hear from you.

## Contact

Email: [support@jiva.studio](mailto:support@jiva.studio)

We usually respond within a few business days.

## When writing to us

To help us resolve your issue faster, please include:

- Your device model and operating system version (e.g. iPhone 13, iOS 18.2).
- The app version (you can find it on the Settings screen inside the app).
- A short description of what happened and what you expected.
